Privacy Policy — Matilha Publicador

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Matilha Publicador ("the app") is an internal tool used only by the team that operates the YouTube channels Cão de Pauta, Woof Game News, Caramelo Caótico and Howling Clips ("Matilha"). It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google.

What data the app accesses

Only data of the channels owned by Matilha, after the channel owner signs in with Google and grants access:

  • channel ID and channel title (to confirm the right channel was selected);
  • videos, thumbnails, captions, playlist items and comments that the app itself uploads or posts on those channels.

The app does not read, collect or store data of any other YouTube user, viewer or commenter.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to upload, schedule and manage videos on our own channels.
  • The OAuth tokens are stored only on the operator's computer, in a protected local folder. No server, database or third party receives them.
  • We do not sell, share, transfer or use the data for advertising or any other purpose.
  • The app uses no cookies and no tracking.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the app is in use and are deleted when access is no longer needed. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation, the app can no longer access the channel.
